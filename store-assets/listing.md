## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
FinBooks is a simple but real double-entry accounting app for small businesses.
Keep your books, send invoices, track bills, and see clear financial reports —
all from your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Dashboard — cash, receivables, payables and this month's profit at a glance,
  with an income-vs-expense chart.
• Invoices (Sales) — create, edit, post to the ledger, record payments, and void.
• Bills (Purchases) — the same, for what you owe your vendors.
• Customers & Vendors — with contact and bank/beneficiary details.
• Vouchers & Journal — receipt, payment and journal vouchers, plus a full
  general ledger for every account.
• Reports — Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, AR/AP Aging, Tax,
  cost-centre and project profit, and a financial-analysis view with key ratios
  and a health score.
• Opening balances, tax rates, cost centres and projects.
• Multi-company — switch between the companies you belong to.
• Roles — Admin, Accountant and Viewer, so your team sees only what they should.
• Export — share invoices/bills as PDF and reports as CSV.
• Notifications — stay updated on activity, even when the app is closed.

BUILT ON SOLID ACCOUNTING
Every transaction posts a balanced journal entry, and all reports are computed
from the general ledger — so your numbers always add up.

FinBooks contains no ads and no third-party tracking.
```
