Delete Your Account & Data

FinBooks · Developer: Ali Sharyar Khan · Last updated: 14 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your FinBooks account and the data associated with it. FinBooks is a bookkeeping and accounting app for small businesses.

How to request deletion

Send an account-deletion request by email:

  1. Email alishehiryarniazi@gmail.com from the email address registered to your FinBooks account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my FinBooks account.
  3. Include the email address of the account you want deleted.

We verify the request against the registered email and complete the deletion, then confirm by email. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your personal account information — your name, email address, and password hash.
  • Your push-notification token.
  • The business/accounting data in any company you solely own — customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, journal entries, chart of accounts, tax rates, and company profile.

What may be kept

  • If you share a company with other members, that company's shared business records remain available to the other members; only your personal membership and personal data are removed.
  • Limited records we are required to retain to comply with legal or tax obligations may be kept for the period required by law, after which they are deleted.
  • Routine backups are purged on our normal backup rotation (within 90 days).
